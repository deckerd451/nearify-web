        What is Nearify?

        Nearify is a live connection layer for in-person events. It helps you see who's around you, discover relevant people, and turn event interactions into lasting connections.

        Live discovery

        See who is actually at this event in real time — not a static attendee list from somewhere else.

        Intentional connection

        Find people who share your goals — whether you're hiring, exploring ideas, or meeting new people.

        Persistent value

        Your interactions become a persistent network. The event doesn't end when you leave.
