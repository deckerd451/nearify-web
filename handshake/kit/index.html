Handshakes event kit

Everything to try Handshakes at a meetup: a slide, table cards to print, and 20 seconds to say from the front.

1. The slide

Put it up during breaks and at the end. Full screen works best.

Before you leave tonight

Scan to connect with the people you meet

Only people you've met can reach you.

nearify.org/handshake

2. Table cards

Prints two folding cards on one sheet of letter paper. Cut along the middle, fold each along its dotted line, and stand it on a table.

3. What to say (about 20 seconds)

"Before you leave, scan the code on the screen and make a card. It takes 20 seconds, with no account. When you meet someone, show them your code. You're only connected if you both say yes. And if you need help with something, post an ask. It goes to the people you met tonight, and they can pass it on."

4. On the night

  • Make your own card before people arrive, so you can show how it works.
  • Say the script once at the start and once before people leave.
  • Connect with a few people yourself. Seeing an organizer do it is the best invitation.
  • The next day, check the Handshakes totals on the dashboard. They're daily counts with nothing about who.
