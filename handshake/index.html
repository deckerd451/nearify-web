      Settings and privacy
      Edit your card

      Shared only with people you connect with. Once they save it, it's theirs. Removing it here stops sharing it, but can't take it off phones it's already saved to.

      What we can see
      • Your card and the people you connect with are kept only in this browser.
      • Our server passes along sealed messages between phones. It can't read them, doesn't know whose they are, and deletes each one when it's picked up or after 7 days.
      • We never see who met whom. Nobody can find you or message you unless you've connected.
      • Asks are sealed too. Someone two steps away never sees your name, only that someone their connection met is asking.
      • How to reach you is never on the code people scan. It's sent, sealed, only to people you've connected with.
      • Saying no, or cancelling, sends nothing. Nobody is told.
      • We count daily totals, like how many cards were made or asks sent, so we know if this is useful. Just a number per day, nothing about who.
      Back up, move to a new phone, or delete

      If this browser clears its storage, your connections go with it. A backup is locked with a passphrase only you know.
